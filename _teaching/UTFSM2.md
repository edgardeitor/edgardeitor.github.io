I taught undergraduate courses in *Algebra and Geometry* (~40 students), *Single-* and *Multivariable Differential Calculus* (~40 students), and *Single-* and *Multivariable Integral Calculus* (~40 students).

Across my Engineering Teacher positions at Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez and Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María, I developed LaTeX-based teaching and assessment materials, including collections of examination questions, additional exercises, and worked solutions. I also organised voluntary weekly problem-solving sessions, sharing problems with students in advance so that they could attempt them independently. At these meetings, we worked through the problems collectively, with me providing detailed explanations and addressing students' questions and difficulties.

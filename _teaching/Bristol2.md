**Postgraduate level.** I was Unit Director for *Mathematical Modelling in Biology, Medicine and Public Health* (~15 students), where I designed lectures and assessments, and coordinated the teaching team. I also taught across MSc programmes in Data Science, Engineering Mathematics, and Financial Technology with Data Science. In 2025, I co-led half of *Introduction to AI and Data Analytics* (~60 students) and designed its coursework. My other teaching contributions include *Visual Analytics* (~250 students), *Software Development: Programming and Algorithms* (~40 students), *Statistical Computing and Empirical Methods*, and *Large-Scale Data Engineering for Business*.

**Undergraduate level.** I taught *Engineering Mathematics 1*, a course serving approximately 1,050 students, and contributed to *Engineering Mathematics 2* (approximately 850 students), *Data Engineering at Scale*, and *Data Science and Applied Statistics*. In addition, I assessed student project work in two courses: for *Introduction to Computer Programming*, I reviewed 17 undergraduate coding projects and questioned students about their code and implementation choices when they presented their work; for *Mathematical and Data Modelling*, I supervised 3 group projects proposed by industry partners and marked their reports. Also, for *Discrete Mathematics*, I developed three sets of supplementary materials based on textbook questions: one containing deliberately incorrect proofs, another containing corrected versions, and a third providing complete, correct solutions. These materials helped students identify errors in mathematical arguments, understand how to correct them, and construct rigorous proofs.

Alongside my teaching, I supervised MSc projects in Financial Technology with Data Science and served as a personal tutor for undergraduate and master's students, helping them adapt to different educational systems and assessment expectations.

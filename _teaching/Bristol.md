**Postgraduate level.** Throughout this period, I supported *Data Science Mini Project* (~100 students), serving as Lead Teaching Assistant in 2022. In this MSc pre-dissertation course, I supervised group research projects proposed by industry partners and marked 10 project reports. In 2023, I co-led the course with an incoming lecturer, whom I supported throughout its delivery. I also assisted with the courses *Software Development: Programming and Algorithms* and *Visual Analytics*.

**Undergraduate level.** My teaching assistance covered *Data Science and Applied Statistics* (~60 students), *Introduction to Data Science*, *Perspectives in Mathematics*, *Mathematical and Data Modelling*, *Introduction to Computer Programming*, *Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos* (~45 students), *Numerical Methods in MATLAB* (~60 students), *Applied Linear Algebra*, *Engineering Mathematics 1*, *Engineering Mathematics 2*, and *Discrete Mathematics*. I also supported final-year BSc students with their dissertations through the *Final Year Project* course.

In addition, I contributed to the Advanced Mathematics Support Programme's *Problem Solving Matters* course, which aims to develop Year 12 students' mathematical thinking and problem-solving skills.

I supported undergraduate and postgraduate teaching across mathematics, nonlinear dynamics, programming, numerical methods, statistics, and data science. For the MSc course Data Science Mini Project (approximately 100 students), I supervised industry-sponsored group research projects and served as lead Teaching Assistant in 2022.

Other teaching included Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos, Numerical Methods in MATLAB, Applied Linear Algebra, Engineering Mathematics 1 and 2, Data Science and Applied Statistics, and Software Development: Programming and Algorithms. I also supported the Advanced Mathematics Support Programme’s Problem Solving Matters course, which aims to develop Year 12 students' mathematical thinking and problem-solving skills.

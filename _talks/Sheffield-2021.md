**Abstract**: It has been proven that many different real-world models can show Turing instabilities over time. These patterns catch the interest of many biologists, ecologists, mathematicians, among others, because their existence reveals conditions under which some species show periodic shapes in space. The problem is that, up until now, the conditions for them to occur in a general system are not well-known. My talk will be about general necessary and sufficient conditions for an equilibrium point of a system of n variables to go through a Turing bifurcation. Moreover, I will give sufficient conditions for an equilibrium point to go through a Turing-Hopf bifurcation, which implies the appearance of periodic spatiotemporal patterns. Finally, I will provide an insight on how to choose diffusion rates in each case to see these patterns when you solve the equations with a suitable initial condition.
